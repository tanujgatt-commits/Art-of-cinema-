BEHIND EVERY SCENE

What Exactly Is a Screenplay?

A screenplay is the blueprint of a film. It describes the story, characters, scenes, actions, dialogue and the progression of events that eventually become a movie.

We notice the actors, music, visuals and direction. But the screenplay quietly connects them all.

01

Story Structure

The screenplay decides how the story begins, develops and reaches its climax.

Beginning → Conflict → Climax → Resolution
02

Character

Great characters are not created only by actors. Their goals, fears, choices and transformations are built into the screenplay.

Want → Struggle → Choice → Change
03

Pacing

The screenplay controls when the audience gets information and when it has to wait.

Fast → Slow → Tension → Release
04

Dialogue

Good dialogue does more than give information. It can reveal personality, conflict and hidden emotions.

What is said ≠ What is meant
05

Suspense

Screenplays can deliberately hide information, reveal clues and create questions that keep the audience watching.

Question → Clue → Doubt → Reveal
06

Emotion

A powerful emotional moment usually works because the screenplay has carefully prepared the audience for it.

Setup → Connection → Payoff

THE INVISIBLE WORK

Why is screenplay
so underrated?

01

It disappears

When a screenplay works perfectly, audiences usually stop thinking about the writing and simply experience the story.

02

Everyone gets the credit

Directors, actors, composers and cinematographers are highly visible. The screenplay is often hidden behind the finished film.

03

Small choices matter

A missing scene, a delayed reveal or one line of dialogue can completely change how an audience experiences a story.
