My Four Favourite Films

Cinema is more than just entertainment. Some films stay with us because of their characters, storytelling, atmosphere and unforgettable moments. Here are four of my favourite films.

1. Businessman

Director: Puri Jagannadh

Language: Telugu

Businessman is a Telugu action film starring Mahesh Babu. I like its powerful dialogues, intense character and fast-paced storytelling.

2. Inglourious Basterds

Director: Quentin Tarantino

Language: English

Inglourious Basterds is a war film known for its unique storytelling, memorable characters and brilliant dialogue.

I like this film because Quentin Tarantino creates tension through conversations and gives every character a strong personality.

3. Heat

Director: Michael Mann

Language: English

Heat is a crime thriller about a professional criminal and a dedicated police detective.

I love this film for its atmosphere, characters, cinematography and realistic portrayal of crime and investigation.

4. The Silence of the Lambs

Director: Jonathan Demme

Language: English

The Silence of the Lambs is a psychological thriller featuring Clarice Starling and Hannibal Lecter.

I like this film because of its suspenseful atmosphere, intelligent dialogue and unforgettable characters.

Why These Films?

These four films are very different from one another, but they all show what makes cinema special. From the mass appeal of Businessman to the tension of Heat, the unique storytelling of Inglourious Basterds and the psychological suspense of The Silence of the Lambs, each film offers a different cinematic experience.
